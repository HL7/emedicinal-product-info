SUMMARY OF PRODUCT CHARACTERISTICS

Summary of Product Characteristics: WonderDrug 500 mg tablets

1. NAME OF THE MEDICINAL PRODUCT

WonderDrug 500 mg tablets

2. QUALITATIVE AND QUANTITATIVE COMPOSITION

Each tablet contains 500 mg paracetamol.

Excipient with known effect: each tablet contains 25 mg lactose (as monohydrate).

For the full list of excipients, see section 6.1.

3. PHARMACEUTICAL FORM

Tablet.

White, round, flat tablet, scored on one side and marked WD500 on the other. The score line is only there to help break the tablet if you have difficulty swallowing it whole.

4. CLINICAL PARTICULARS

See sections 4.1 to 4.9.

4.1 Therapeutic indications

WonderDrug is indicated for the symptomatic treatment of mild to moderate pain and fever in adults and children aged 12 years and over.

4.2 Posology and method of administration

Adults and adolescents aged 16 years and over, weighing 50 kg or more: 1 or 2 tablets every 4 to 6 hours as needed. Do not take more than 8 tablets in 24 hours.

Adolescents aged 12 to 15 years, weighing 40 to 49 kg: 1 tablet every 4 to 6 hours as needed. Do not take more than 4 tablets in 24 hours.

Leave at least 4 hours between doses. Not for children under 12 years.

Method of administration: oral use. Swallow the tablets with water.

4.3 Contraindications

Hypersensitivity to paracetamol or to any of the excipients listed in section 6.1.

Severe hepatic impairment.

4.4 Special warnings and precautions for use

Do not take with any other medicine that contains paracetamol. Taking more than the recommended dose can cause serious liver damage.

This medicine contains lactose. Patients with rare hereditary problems of galactose intolerance, total lactase deficiency or glucose-galactose malabsorption should not take this medicine.

4.5 Interaction with other medicinal products and other forms of interaction

The anticoagulant effect of warfarin may be increased by prolonged regular daily use of paracetamol, with an increased risk of bleeding. Occasional doses have no significant effect. Monitor the INR during and after prolonged regular use.

4.6 Fertility, pregnancy and lactation

WonderDrug can be used during pregnancy and breast-feeding at the lowest effective dose for the shortest possible time.

4.7 Effects on ability to drive and use machines

WonderDrug has no or negligible influence on the ability to drive and use machines.

4.8 Undesirable effects

Frequency Undesirable effect
Rare (may affect up to 1 in 1,000 people) Skin rash

4.9 Overdose

Overdose can cause liver damage, which may not be apparent for 24 to 48 hours. Patients should seek medical help immediately, even if they feel well. Treatment: acetylcysteine.

5. PHARMACOLOGICAL PROPERTIES

See sections 5.1 to 5.3.

5.1 Pharmacodynamic properties

Pharmacotherapeutic group: other analgesics and antipyretics, anilides. ATC code: N02BE01.

5.2 Pharmacokinetic properties

Paracetamol is rapidly and almost completely absorbed. Peak plasma concentrations are reached 30 to 60 minutes after an oral dose.

5.3 Preclinical safety data

There are no preclinical data of relevance beyond those included in other sections.

6. PHARMACEUTICAL PARTICULARS

See sections 6.1 to 6.6.

6.1 List of excipients

Maize starch, lactose monohydrate, magnesium stearate.

6.2 Incompatibilities

Not applicable.

6.3 Shelf life

3 years.

6.4 Special precautions for storage

Store below 25 °C. Store in the original package to protect from light.

6.5 Nature and contents of container

PVC/aluminium blisters in a cardboard carton. Pack size: 20 tablets (2 blisters of 10 tablets).

6.6 Special precautions for disposal

No special requirements.
