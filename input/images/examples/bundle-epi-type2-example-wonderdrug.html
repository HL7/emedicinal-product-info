SUMMARY OF PRODUCT CHARACTERISTICS

Summary of Product Characteristics: WonderDrug 500 mg tablets

1. NAME OF THE MEDICINAL PRODUCT

WonderDrug 500 mg tablets

🔗 MedicinalProductDefinition: WonderDrug 500 mg tablets

2. QUALITATIVE AND QUANTITATIVE COMPOSITION

Each tablet contains 500 mg paracetamol.

Excipient with known effect: each tablet contains 25 mg lactose (as monohydrate).

For the full list of excipients, see section 6.1.

🔗 Ingredient: Paracetamol🔗 Ingredient: Maize starch🔗 Ingredient: Lactose monohydrate🔗 Ingredient: Magnesium stearate

3. PHARMACEUTICAL FORM

Tablet.

White, round, flat tablet, scored on one side and marked WD500 on the other. The score line is only there to help break the tablet if you have difficulty swallowing it whole.

🔗 ManufacturedItemDefinition: Tablet🔗 AdministrableProductDefinition: Tablet

4. CLINICAL PARTICULARS

See sections 4.1 to 4.9.

4.1 Therapeutic indications

WonderDrug is indicated for the symptomatic treatment of mild to moderate pain and fever in adults and children aged 12 years and over.

4.2 Posology and method of administration

Adults and adolescents aged 16 years and over, weighing 50 kg or more: 1 or 2 tablets every 4 to 6 hours as needed. Do not take more than 8 tablets in 24 hours.

Adolescents aged 12 to 15 years, weighing 40 to 49 kg: 1 tablet every 4 to 6 hours as needed. Do not take more than 4 tablets in 24 hours.

Leave at least 4 hours between doses. Not for children under 12 years.

Method of administration: oral use. Swallow the tablets with water.

4.3 Contraindications

Hypersensitivity to paracetamol or to any of the excipients listed in section 6.1.

Severe hepatic impairment.

4.4 Special warnings and precautions for use

Do not take with any other medicine that contains paracetamol. Taking more than the recommended dose can cause serious liver damage.

This medicine contains lactose. Patients with rare hereditary problems of galactose intolerance, total lactase deficiency or glucose-galactose malabsorption should not take this medicine.

4.5 Interaction with other medicinal products and other forms of interaction

The anticoagulant effect of warfarin may be increased by prolonged regular daily use of paracetamol, with an increased risk of bleeding. Occasional doses have no significant effect. Monitor the INR during and after prolonged regular use.

4.6 Fertility, pregnancy and lactation

WonderDrug can be used during pregnancy and breast-feeding at the lowest effective dose for the shortest possible time.

4.7 Effects on ability to drive and use machines

WonderDrug has no or negligible influence on the ability to drive and use machines.

4.8 Undesirable effects

FrequencyUndesirable effect
Rare (may affect up to 1 in 1,000 people)Skin rash

4.9 Overdose

Overdose can cause liver damage, which may not be apparent for 24 to 48 hours. Patients should seek medical help immediately, even if they feel well. Treatment: acetylcysteine.

5. PHARMACOLOGICAL PROPERTIES

See sections 5.1 to 5.3.

5.1 Pharmacodynamic properties

Pharmacotherapeutic group: other analgesics and antipyretics, anilides. ATC code: N02BE01.

5.2 Pharmacokinetic properties

Paracetamol is rapidly and almost completely absorbed. Peak plasma concentrations are reached 30 to 60 minutes after an oral dose.

5.3 Preclinical safety data

There are no preclinical data of relevance beyond those included in other sections.

6. PHARMACEUTICAL PARTICULARS

See sections 6.1 to 6.6.

6.1 List of excipients

Maize starch, lactose monohydrate, magnesium stearate.

🔗 Ingredient: Maize starch🔗 Ingredient: Lactose monohydrate🔗 Ingredient: Magnesium stearate

6.2 Incompatibilities

Not applicable.

6.3 Shelf life

3 years.

6.4 Special precautions for storage

Store below 25 °C. Store in the original package to protect from light.

6.5 Nature and contents of container

PVC/aluminium blisters in a cardboard carton. Pack size: 20 tablets (2 blisters of 10 tablets).

🔗 PackagedProductDefinition: WonderDrug 500 mg tablets, carton of 20 (2 blisters of 10 tablets)

6.6 Special precautions for disposal

No special requirements.

PACKAGE LEAFLET: INFORMATION FOR THE USER

WonderDrug 500 mg tablets

paracetamol

Read all of this leaflet carefully before you start taking this medicine because it contains important information for you.

  • Keep this leaflet. You may need to read it again.
  • Ask your pharmacist if you need more information or advice.
  • If you get any side effects, talk to your doctor or pharmacist. This includes any possible side effects not listed in this leaflet. See section 4.
  • You must talk to a doctor if you do not feel better or if you feel worse after 3 days.

1. What WonderDrug is and what it is used for

WonderDrug contains paracetamol, which belongs to a group of medicines called pain relievers (analgesics) and fever reducers (antipyretics).

It is used to treat mild to moderate pain, such as headache, toothache and period pain, and to reduce fever, in adults and children aged 12 years and over.

2. What you need to know before you take WonderDrug

Read the sections below before you take this medicine.

Do not take WonderDrug

  • if you are allergic to paracetamol or any of the other ingredients of this medicine (listed in section 6).
  • if you have severe liver problems.

Warnings and precautions

Talk to your doctor or pharmacist before taking WonderDrug if you have liver or kidney problems, or if you regularly drink alcohol.

Do not take more than the recommended dose. Taking too much paracetamol can cause serious liver damage.

Do not take WonderDrug with any other medicine that contains paracetamol.

Children

Do not give this medicine to children under 12 years. A suspension (WonderDrug 250 mg/5 mL powder for oral suspension) is available for children aged 6 to 12 years.

Other medicines and WonderDrug

Tell your doctor or pharmacist if you are taking, have recently taken or might take any other medicines, in particular warfarin or other medicines to thin the blood.

Pregnancy and breast-feeding

If you are pregnant or breast-feeding, think you may be pregnant or are planning to have a baby, ask your doctor or pharmacist for advice before taking this medicine. If necessary, WonderDrug can be used during pregnancy and breast-feeding. Use the lowest dose that reduces your pain or fever and use it for the shortest time possible.

Driving and using machines

WonderDrug is not expected to affect your ability to drive or use machines.

WonderDrug contains lactose

If you have been told by your doctor that you have an intolerance to some sugars, contact your doctor before taking this medicine.

3. How to take WonderDrug

Always take this medicine exactly as described in this leaflet or as your doctor or pharmacist has told you. Check with your doctor or pharmacist if you are not sure.

AgeDoseMaximum in 24 hours
Adults and adolescents aged 16 years and over (50 kg or more)1 or 2 tablets8 tablets
Adolescents aged 12 to 15 years (40 to 49 kg)1 tablet4 tablets

Leave at least 4 hours between doses. Swallow the tablets whole with a glass of water.

If you take more WonderDrug than you should

Talk to a doctor at once if you take too much of this medicine, even if you feel well. Too much paracetamol can cause delayed, serious liver damage.

If you forget to take WonderDrug

Do not take a double dose to make up for a forgotten dose. Take the next dose when needed, leaving at least 4 hours since the last dose.

4. Possible side effects

Like all medicines, this medicine can cause side effects, although not everybody gets them.

Rare (may affect up to 1 in 1,000 people): skin rash. Stop taking WonderDrug and tell your doctor straight away if you get a rash.

Reporting of side effects

If you get any side effects, talk to your doctor or pharmacist. You can also report side effects directly via the national reporting system. By reporting side effects you can help provide more information on the safety of this medicine.

5. How to store WonderDrug

  • Keep this medicine out of the sight and reach of children.
  • Do not use this medicine after the expiry date which is stated on the carton and blister after EXP.
  • Store below 25 °C. Store in the original package to protect from light.
  • Do not throw away any medicines via wastewater or household waste. Ask your pharmacist how to throw away medicines you no longer use.

6. Contents of the pack and other information

See the sections below.

What WonderDrug contains

  • The active substance is paracetamol. Each tablet contains 500 mg paracetamol.
  • The other ingredients are maize starch, lactose monohydrate and magnesium stearate.

What WonderDrug looks like and contents of the pack

White, round, flat tablets, scored on one side and marked WD500 on the other. Packs of 20 tablets (2 blisters of 10 tablets).
