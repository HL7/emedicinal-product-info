SUMMARY OF PRODUCT CHARACTERISTICS

Summary of Product Characteristics: WonderDrug 250 mg/5 mL powder for oral suspension

1. NAME OF THE MEDICINAL PRODUCT

WonderDrug 250 mg/5 mL powder for oral suspension

🔗 MedicinalProductDefinition: WonderDrug 250 mg/5 mL powder for oral suspension

2. QUALITATIVE AND QUANTITATIVE COMPOSITION

Each bottle contains 5 g paracetamol. After reconstitution, each 5 mL of oral suspension contains 250 mg paracetamol.

Excipients with known effect: each 5 mL contains 2.5 g sucrose and 5 mg sodium benzoate (E211).

For the full list of excipients, see section 6.1.

🔗 Ingredient: Paracetamol🔗 Ingredient: Sucrose🔗 Ingredient: Xanthan gum🔗 Ingredient: Sodium benzoate🔗 Ingredient: Orange flavour

3. PHARMACEUTICAL FORM

Powder for oral suspension. White to off-white powder.

After reconstitution: a white, orange-flavoured oral suspension.

🔗 ManufacturedItemDefinition: Powder for oral suspension🔗 AdministrableProductDefinition: Oral suspension

4. CLINICAL PARTICULARS

See sections 4.1 and 4.2.

4.1 Therapeutic indications

WonderDrug is indicated for the symptomatic treatment of mild to moderate pain and fever in children aged 6 to 12 years.

4.2 Posology and method of administration

AgeDose of reconstituted suspension
6 to 7 years5 mL (250 mg)
8 to 9 years7.5 mL (375 mg)
10 to 12 years10 mL (500 mg)

Give the dose every 4 to 6 hours as needed. Do not give more than 4 doses in 24 hours. Use the oral syringe provided to measure the dose.

6. PHARMACEUTICAL PARTICULARS

See sections 6.1 to 6.6.

6.1 List of excipients

Sucrose, xanthan gum, sodium benzoate (E211), orange flavour.

🔗 Ingredient: Sucrose🔗 Ingredient: Xanthan gum🔗 Ingredient: Sodium benzoate🔗 Ingredient: Orange flavour

6.3 Shelf life

Unopened: 3 years.

After reconstitution: 14 days.

6.4 Special precautions for storage

Store below 25 °C. After reconstitution, store in a refrigerator (2 °C to 8 °C).

6.5 Nature and contents of container

High-density polyethylene bottle with a child-resistant polypropylene cap, containing powder to make 100 mL of oral suspension. Each carton also contains a 5 mL oral syringe graduated in 0.25 mL steps.

🔗 PackagedProductDefinition: WonderDrug 250 mg/5 mL powder for oral suspension, 1 bottle (100 mL after reconstitution) with 5 mL oral syringe

6.6 Special precautions for disposal and other handling

Instructions for reconstitution: tap the bottle to loosen the powder. Add 90 mL of water up to the mark on the bottle label, close the bottle and shake well for 1 minute. This makes 100 mL of oral suspension. Shake the bottle before each use.

🔗 AdministrableProductDefinition: Oral suspension

PACKAGE LEAFLET: INFORMATION FOR THE PATIENT OR CAREGIVER

WonderDrug 250 mg/5 mL powder for oral suspension

paracetamol

Read all of this leaflet carefully before you give this medicine to your child because it contains important information.

  • Keep this leaflet. You may need to read it again.
  • Ask your pharmacist if you need more information or advice.
  • If your child gets any side effects, talk to your doctor or pharmacist. See section 4.
  • You must talk to a doctor if your child does not feel better or feels worse after 3 days.

1. What WonderDrug suspension is and what it is used for

WonderDrug suspension contains paracetamol, a pain reliever and fever reducer. It is used to treat mild to moderate pain and fever in children aged 6 to 12 years.

2. What you need to know before you give WonderDrug suspension

Read the sections below before you give this medicine.

Do not give WonderDrug suspension

  • if your child is allergic to paracetamol or any of the other ingredients of this medicine (listed in section 6).
  • if your child has severe liver problems.

Warnings and precautions

Do not give more than the recommended dose. Do not give this medicine with any other medicine that contains paracetamol.

WonderDrug suspension contains sucrose and sodium benzoate

Each 5 mL contains 2.5 g sucrose. This should be taken into account in patients with diabetes mellitus. May be harmful to the teeth. If your doctor has told you that your child has an intolerance to some sugars, contact your doctor before giving this medicine.

This medicine contains 5 mg sodium benzoate (E211) in each 5 mL.

3. How to give WonderDrug suspension

Shake the bottle well before each use. Use the oral syringe in the pack to measure the dose. See the Instructions for use for how to prepare the suspension and use the syringe.

AgeDose
6 to 7 years5 mL
8 to 9 years7.5 mL
10 to 12 years10 mL

Give the dose every 4 to 6 hours as needed. Do not give more than 4 doses in 24 hours.

If you give more WonderDrug suspension than you should

Talk to a doctor at once if your child takes too much of this medicine, even if your child seems well.

4. Possible side effects

Like all medicines, this medicine can cause side effects, although not everybody gets them. Rare (may affect up to 1 in 1,000 people): skin rash.

5. How to store WonderDrug suspension

  • Keep this medicine out of the sight and reach of children.
  • Before mixing: store below 25 °C.
  • After mixing: store in a refrigerator (2 °C to 8 °C) and use within 14 days.

6. Contents of the pack and other information

Each 5 mL of mixed suspension contains 250 mg paracetamol. The other ingredients are sucrose, xanthan gum, sodium benzoate (E211) and orange flavour. Each carton contains a bottle of powder to make 100 mL of suspension and a 5 mL oral syringe.

Marketing authorization holder and manufacturer: WonderPharma Ltd., 1 Example Street, Dublin 2, D02 X123, Ireland.

INSTRUCTIONS FOR USE

WonderDrug 250 mg/5 mL powder for oral suspension

Read these instructions before you prepare the suspension and each time before you give a dose. Use only the 5 mL oral syringe that comes with this medicine.

What is in the pack

PartWhat it is for
Bottle with powderMakes 100 mL of suspension after you add water
Child-resistant capPush down and turn to open
Bottle adapterHolds the syringe in the bottle
5 mL oral syringeMeasures the dose, marked every 0.25 mL

Prepare the suspension (pharmacist or caregiver, first use only)

  1. Tap the closed bottle to loosen the powder.
  2. Add drinking water up to the fill mark on the label (90 mL).
  3. Close the bottle and shake well for 1 minute.
  4. Press the bottle adapter into the neck of the bottle.
  5. Write the date of mixing on the label. Throw away any suspension left after 14 days.

Measure and give a dose

  1. Shake the bottle well before each dose.
  2. Insert the tip of the empty syringe into the bottle adapter and turn the bottle upside down.
  3. Draw up the dose: pull the plunger slowly until the top edge of the plunger lines up with the mark for your child's dose (see the table below). If you see air bubbles, push the suspension back and draw up again.
  4. Give the dose: place the syringe tip inside your child's cheek and push the plunger slowly, so your child can swallow.
AgeFill the syringe to
6 to 7 years5 mL (1 full syringe)
8 to 9 years7.5 mL (1 full syringe, then 2.5 mL)
10 to 12 years10 mL (2 full syringes)

Clean and store

  • Leave the adapter in the bottle and close the cap after each use.
  • Pull the syringe apart, rinse both parts in warm water and let them air dry.
  • Store the bottle in a refrigerator (2 °C to 8 °C).

Questions

If you have questions about how to use the syringe, ask your pharmacist or contact WonderPharma Ltd. at medinfo@wonderpharma.example.com.
